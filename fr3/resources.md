## What's available

| Division | Materials | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Parasite Resource | Living filarial parasites (multiple life stages), maintained in animal hosts and insect vectors | Shipment quantities are limited by default; exceptions considered for justified need |
| Molecular Resources | Nucleic acids, cell lines, and related molecular reagents | Some materials also available via [BEI Resources](https://www.beiresources.org/Home/FR3.aspx) |
| Protocols & training | Standard operating procedures; hands-on training in parasite/vector management | See [SOPs](/fr3/sops/) |

## How to request materials

1. **Review availability.** Check this page and the
   [BEI Resources FR3 catalog](https://www.beiresources.org/Home/FR3.aspx)
   for materials already listed.
2. **Submit a request.** Contact FR3 directly at
   [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}) with the
   material requested, intended use, and shipping destination.
   <!-- TODO (Elyssa): replace with your actual request form/portal link if you have one. -->
3. **Complete required agreements.** Most material transfers require a
   signed indemnification agreement and/or Materials Transfer Agreement
   (MTA) before shipment.
4. **Shipping costs.** Requesters are typically responsible for shipping
   costs; you'll be notified of these before your request ships.
5. **Acknowledge NIAID.** Any publication resulting from the use of FR3
   materials must acknowledge NIAID support.

## Questions

For questions about whether a specific material or protocol is available,
email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}).
