The **Filariasis Research Reagent Resource Center (FR3)** maintains
oversight of filarial parasites, standard operating procedures, and
molecular reagents, and distributes them to the research community. FR3
is funded by NIAID and directed by {{ site.pi_name }}.

FR3 operates through two divisions:

## Parasite Resource Division

Provides living filarial parasites, sustained through laboratory passage
in animal hosts and insect vectors, along with standard protocols and
hands-on training in parasite and vector management techniques.
Shipment quantities are limited by default, though exceptions can be made
for justified research needs.

## Molecular Resources Division

Provides molecular research materials — nucleic acids, cell lines, and
related reagents — to support genomic, transcriptomic, and molecular
studies of filarial parasites. Some materials are also available directly
through [BEI Resources](https://www.beiresources.org/Home/FR3.aspx).

## Requesting resources

- Materials are distributed through an application process; all
  reasonable requests are considered.
- Requesters may be responsible for shipping costs.
- An indemnification agreement and/or Materials Transfer Agreement (MTA)
  is typically required before materials ship.
- Investigators must acknowledge NIAID in any publication resulting from
  the use of FR3 materials.

See **[Requesting Resources](/fr3/resources/)** for the full process, and
**[SOPs](/fr3/sops/)** for protocols.
