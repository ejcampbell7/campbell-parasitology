We are a research lab in the Department of Infectious Diseases at the
University of Georgia College of Veterinary Medicine, studying how
parasitic nematodes — canine heartworm, the agents of human lymphatic
filariasis, and canine hookworm — establish infection in their hosts.
We combine molecular parasitology, immunology, and genomics/transcriptomics
to understand host specificity and drug resistance, and we work toward
laboratory alternatives to animal models wherever we can.

Our lab also directs **FR3**, the NIH/NIAID-funded Filariasis Research
Reagent Resource Center, which supplies filarial parasites, molecular
reagents, and protocols to researchers around the world.
